Chapter 2

Music and Songwriting

Alex's creative life started with music, well before code or classrooms.

He played trombone in middle school, then picked up the guitar in high school. That turned into songwriting through his college years and his early twenties.

Teaching himself production

Driven by curiosity, he taught himself music theory and production and made several albums of electronic music. The playlist below has his guitar and electronic work in its original form.

Revisiting old songs with AI

More recently, Alex has been revitalizing many of his old songs with AI, to hear what full productions of them would sound like.

He also made My Name Is Alex, a 7 minute 40 second music video that introduces his story. It plays at the top of the homepage. The full catalogue lives at AlexMercedMusic.com.
